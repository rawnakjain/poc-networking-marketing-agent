Salon Owner

Agent Hello! I'm your marketing matchmaking agent. To get started, could you tell me your salon's name and location?

Agent Wonderful! What's your main marketing objective for the upcoming Diwali season?
Salon Owner I want to increase bookings through better Instagram content and targeted ad campaigns, but I need to keep my budget under ₹15,000.

Salon Owner I prefer vibrant, festive posts that showcase our Diwali special packages and bridal makeup services. Short video reels would be great.
Agent How quickly do you need to see results or have content ready?
Salon Owner I'd like to start campaigns in two weeks, so content creation should happen within the next 10 days.
Agent Thank you, Priya. That's all I need from you for now.

How the Matchmaking Process Works

1

Context Gathering

The agent begins by understanding the salon's specific needs:
• Salon name, location, and type
• Marketing objectives (Diwali campaigns)
• Budget constraints (₹15,000/month)
• Content preferences (Instagram, festive, video reels)
• Timeline (start in 2 weeks)
2

Freelancer Evaluation

The agent interviews the marketing freelancer:
• Specialization: Beauty/wellness brands
• Services: Content creation, community management
• Pricing: ₹8,000/month (within budget)
• Availability: Immediate start
• Experience: Proven Diwali campaign success
3

Agency Evaluation

The agent interviews the marketing agency:
• Specialization: Premium lifestyle brands
• Services: Full-service marketing
• Pricing: ₹25,000/month (exceeds budget)
• Capacity: Team of 5
• Expertise: Festive campaigns for premium salons
4

Match Recommendation

Based on the analysis:
• Budget fit: Freelancer (₹8,000) vs Agency (₹25,000)
• Timeline fit: Freelancer can start immediately
• Experience match: Both have Diwali expertise
• Recommendation: Freelancer is the better match
• Next step: Facilitate introduction
